## Post (ready to edit)

We tested the question people keep avoiding:

**Can a stronger AI model eliminate the need for governance?**

Early answer: **no.**

Better models can draft better judgment.  
But the system still needs to check, repair, route, and record before action.

In our staged MaatBench capture on Claude Fable 5 (109 cases across smoke, prompted, and governance-stress tiers):

- Raw covenant behavior improved versus our smaller-model baseline
- Repair burden dropped
- **Unsafe allow stayed at 0%**
- Compiler enforcement was still required for auditable governed records

Training improves the model.  
Prompting improves the draft.  
**The compiler governs the record.**

This was not a full benchmark run. It was early cross-model evidence that the architecture claim holds on a frontier-class model — not just on the model we trained.

More soon on Ma'at judgment records and Tehuti Guard as constitutional enforcement for AI actions.
