- **Source:** National Institute of Standards and Technology. *Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0).* NIST AI 100-1.
- **Date:** January 2023; NIST page says released January 26, 2023.
- **Identifier:** DOI `10.6028/NIST.AI.100-1`.
- **URL:** `https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework`

### NIST Generative AI Profile

- **Source:** Autio, Chloe; Schwartz, Reva; Dunietz, Jesse; Jain, Shomik; Stanley, Martin; Tabassi, Elham; Hall, Patrick; Roberts, Kamie. *Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework: Generative Artificial Intelligence Profile.* NIST Trustworthy and Responsible AI 600-1.
- **Date:** Published July 26, 2024; updated April 8, 2026.
- **Identifier:** DOI `10.6028/NIST.AI.600-1`.
- **URL:** `https://www.nist.gov/publications/artificial-intelligence-risk-management-framework-generative-artificial-intelligence`
- **Use in manuscript:** Strong source for generative-AI-specific risks, content provenance, suggested actions, and mapping to Govern / Map / Measure / Manage.

### NIST Trustworthy AI in Critical Infrastructure Profile

- **Source:** NIST concept note: *Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework: Trustworthy AI in Critical Infrastructure Profile.*
- **Date:** NIST AI RMF page and search result indicate concept note released April 7, 2026.
- **URL:** `https://www.nist.gov/document/concept-note-artificial-intelligence-risk-management-framework-trustworthy-ai-critical`
- **Caveat:** This is a **concept note / profile in development**, not a final standard or compliance requirement. The manuscript should say "NIST has released a concept note for..." rather than implying a finalized profile.
- **Use in manuscript:** Good source for the claim that AI governance is moving into critical-infrastructure framing, but use precise wording.

### UNESCO Recommendation on the Ethics of AI

- **Source:** UNESCO. *Recommendation on the Ethics of Artificial Intelligence.*
- **Date:** Adopted November 2021.
- **URL:** `https://www.unesco.org/en/legal-affairs/recommendation-ethics-artificial-intelligence`
- **Key paragraphs verified:** Responsibility/accountability and human oversight; auditability/traceability; lifecycle accountability and legal/person responsibility.
- **Use in manuscript:** Strong source for human responsibility, oversight, auditability, traceability, and lifecycle accountability.

### OECD AI Principles

- **Source:** OECD. *Recommendation of the Council on Artificial Intelligence*, OECD/LEGAL/0449.
- **Date:** Adopted 22 May 2019; updated in 2024.
- **URL:** `https://legalinstruments.oecd.org/en/instruments/OECD-LEGAL-0449`
- **Use in manuscript:** Strong source for human-centred values, fairness, transparency/explainability, robustness/security/safety, and accountability.

### OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications

- **Source:** OWASP GenAI Security Project. *OWASP Top 10 for Large Language Model Applications* (2025).
