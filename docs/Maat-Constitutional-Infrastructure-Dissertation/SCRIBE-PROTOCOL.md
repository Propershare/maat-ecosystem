# Ma’at Scribe Protocol (Dissertation Edition)

A minimal constitutional system for preserving truth, order, and accountability in your work.

## Core principle

**You cannot fix what you do not understand—and you cannot understand what you cannot trace.**

This protocol ensures the dissertation remains:

- **Truthful** (provenance)
- **Ordered** (structure)
- **Balanced** (signal over noise)
- **Accountable** (traceable decisions)

---

## I. The three-layer writing system (do not collapse)

### 1. Raw chronicle (research layer)

**Purpose:** Capture reality as it happens.

**Content:** notes, ideas, quotes, rough thoughts, conversations.

**Rule:** Nothing here is automatically true—only recorded.

### 2. Curated memory (decision layer)

**Purpose:** Record what you decided and why.

Each entry should answer: **What changed?** **Why?** **What evidence supports it?**

**Rule:** This is where thinking becomes structured knowledge.

### 3. Canonical manuscript (truth layer)

**Purpose:** The dissertation itself—clean, structured, argued, citation-ready.

**Rule:** Only promote ideas here after they are clarified and justified.

---

## II. The Ma’at check (before anything becomes canon)

Before adding to the manuscript, ask:

| Principle | Question |
|-----------|----------|
| **Truth** | Can I trace where this claim comes from? |
| **Order** | Does this belong in this section? |
| **Balance** | Is this necessary, or only interesting? |
| **Justice** | Am I representing sources and ideas accurately? |
| **Accountability** | Could I defend this under questioning? |

If **two or more** fail → it does not enter the manuscript.

---

## III. Folder layout (this lab)

See `index.md` and:

| Path | Role |
|------|------|
| `manuscript/draft.md` | Pointer + rules for the single authoritative draft |
| `notes/raw_notes.md` | Raw chronicle |
| `decisions/decisions_log.md` | Curated decisions |
| `index.md` | Public anchor (“what is true now”) |

Legacy filenames (`Maat-Constitutional-Infrastructure-Dissertation.md`, blueprint, PDFs) remain alongside until you consolidate.

---

## IV. Index file

The **index** is the two-minute orientation for any reader. Keep it current.

---

## V. Rhythm

- **Daily (5–10 min):** add to raw notes; log real decisions.
- **Weekly (optional):** clean one section; remove duplication.

---

## VI. What not to do

- Treat chat logs as final truth.
- Put everything in one undifferentiated file.
- Over-document instead of writing.
- Build a perfect system before finishing.
- Let ideas enter the manuscript without challenge.

---

## VII. Role: the scribe

**Archivist** → preserves traceability · **Editor** → clarity · **Judge** → filters weak claims · **Builder** → the final argument.

---

## VIII. Alignment with the thesis

| Ma’at principle | Dissertation practice |
|-----------------|------------------------|
| Truth | Provenance of claims |
| Order | Structured chapters |
| Balance | Cut the unnecessary |
| Justice | Accurate representation |
| Accountability | Defensible argument |

**Bottom line:** You need clear separation of layers, disciplined promotion of ideas, and one clean manuscript—not a complex lab system to finish.
