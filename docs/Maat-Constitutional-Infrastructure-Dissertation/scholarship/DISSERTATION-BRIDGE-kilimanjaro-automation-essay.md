# Dissertation bridge — Kilimanjaro automation essay ↔ Ma'at constitutional infrastructure

**Purpose:** Tie Dr. Tdka Kilimanjaro's political-economy essay (AI, wage labor, structural crisis, youth pedagogy) to the dissertation *Ma'at as Constitutional Infrastructure* without collapsing layers.

**Rule:** Political economy = **why power moves**. Constitutional infrastructure = **how governed systems must be built**. Both required; neither replaces the other.

---

## Thesis alignment (one paragraph)

The dissertation argues advanced AI is **organized power** requiring **constitutional infrastructure** (truth, balance, order, justice, reciprocity, accountability). Kilimanjaro's essay argues advanced automation may be **organized power against wage-labor capitalism itself**, producing structural crisis, scapegoating, and a demand for rigorous youth education. Together: the same technological shift that threatens mass purchasing power and Black survival **also** concentrates compute, data, and agentic action in few hands — so Ma'at-governed infrastructure is not decorative ethics but **institutional defense** in a transition period.
