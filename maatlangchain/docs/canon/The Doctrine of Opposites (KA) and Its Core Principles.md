# The Doctrine of Opposites (KA) and Its Core Principles

## Kmtian Understanding
- Every being or force possesses an internal duality — its **KA** — which animates life and movement. The KA is both the **self** and **opposite self**, inseparable yet opposed.
- All existence begins with dual poles — day/night, masculine/feminine, sky/earth, Upper/Lower Kmt, red/white, Osirian/Solar papyrus. The dynamism between them gives rise to life.
- When two opposites reach the point of maximum tension, they resolve through regeneration — becoming a **new unity** (symbolized by the double crown).
- Regression is **not** death — it is a womb of **rebirth** (seen in Asar and Heru cycles).

## Philosophical Process
- The unity and struggle of opposites **within the same being** is resolved by **transformation** — death and rebirth (negation of the negation).
- Destruction and renewal form a cycle; no end is **final**, every death is a **seed for rebirth**.
- Every cycle in nature mirrors this rhythm.

## Greek/European View
- Greek logic, especially Aristotle’s, emphasizes fixed categories. Opposites are external.
- Western dualism treats death as final; negation without rebirth.
- Lacks synthesis: one must dominate the other (e.g., Being vs. Non-Being).
