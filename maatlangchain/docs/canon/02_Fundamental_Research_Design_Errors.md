# Fundamental Research Design Errors

| Problem Area          | Problem Nature                                             | Solution or Diagnostic                                                                 |
|-----------------------|------------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Design Selection       | Cross-sectional versus longitudinal                        | Does design satisfy inferential requirements of the hypothesis? Is medium appropriate to topic, design, precision requirements? |
| Sampling Error         | Random misestimation of population value by sample         | Is the sample size adequate to provide precision in the required confidence range? (Check sampling error) |
| Sampling Bias          | Constant misestimation of population value by sample       | Is the sample selected randomly from representative sampling frame?                    |
| Data Collection Error  | Interviewers make random reading or recording mistakes     | Are interviewers trained and is questionnaire well formatted? Are items clear, well scaled, and sufficient in number? (Check reliability measurement) |
| Data Collection Bias   | Data are miscoded or carelessly managed                    | Are data checked for accuracy? Is completion rate high? Do incomplete or refusals differ from the complete? Are interviewers trained to avoid bias? Are items worded neutrally and ordered to avoid bias? |
