# KMT Firsts Chronology

| Description from *KMT Firsts* | Revised Chronology Placement | Evidence | Historical Significance |
|-----------------------------|-----------------------------|---------|-------------------------|
| Written language: literary papyri (poems, epics, philosophy, satire, fairy tales); use of life as libraries. | Proto-Unification: Narmer (4308–4268 BC) to Early Dynastic (Dyn. 1–2; 4265–3698 BC) | Narmer Palette, Abydos labels, Pyramid Texts. | Africa’s foundational literacy system. |
| Cloths colored inks with chemical knowledge. | Early Dynastic Period (4265–3698 BC) | Ink recipes preserved in papyri. | Critical to written communication. |
| Spinning cloth – linen, chemical dyes and bleaches. | Naqada → Early Dynastic (before 4200 BC onward). | Linen wrappings in tombs. | Africa’s first mass textile production. |
| Astronomical alignment of pyramids; Sirius Calendar; current-day calendar (4236 BC), temples, pylons, obelisks. | Sirius Calendar: Narmer/Aha-Menes (~4306–4200 BC). Pyramids: Old Kingdom II, Djoser (3675–3646 BC) → Khufu (3460–3397 BC), Senemut under Hatshepsut (1479–1459 BC). | Star alignments, Calendar tomb. | First solar calendar still in use. |
| Paved first road (~4700 years ago). | Old Kingdom II, Djoser–Sneferu (3675–3489 BC). | Quarry roads at Wadi Hammamat. | Enabled transport of basalt for pyramids. |
| Mass-produced writing sheets. | Old Kingdom II, 3rd–4th Dynasties (3675–3397 BC). | Surviving papyri (medical). | Spread literacy globally. |
| Paper water clocks, merkhets, obelisks. | Middle Kingdom: 12th Dynasty (2235–2022 BC). | Sundials from Valley of Kings. | Standardized time for ceremony & ritual. |
| Pyramid, temples, pylons, obelisks. | Age of Pyramid Builders: Sneferu (3515–3489 BC), Khufu (3460–3397 BC), Khafra (3387–3321 BC), Menkaura (3321–2258 BC). | Pyramids of Giza; Karnak obelisks. | Benchmark in monumental architecture. |
| Pyramids: 6 million tons, 485 ft high. | Khufu (3460–3397 BC). | Great Pyramid itself. | Largest single human construction. |
| Hoe: 18,000 BP; ox plow ~3100 BC. | Proto-Unification (before 4500 BC), Aha-Menes (~4268–4200 BC). | Oldest farming tools, plow remains. | African farming revolution. |
| Earliest maps (Giza Kingdom); gold mine maps; land ownership. | Coffin maps: Middle Kingdom (2235–1743 BC). Gold maps: New Kingdom 19th Dynasty (Rameses II, 1209–1223 BC). | Wadi Hammamat map. | First mining records. |
| Water wheels and irrigation. | Old Kingdom I–II (3465–3200 BC). | Wadi irrigation works. | Manage Nile flood water. |
| Ramps, inclined planes. | Old Kingdom Pyramid Age (3675–2600 BC). | Ramp remains near pyramids. | Stone transport. |
| Large-scale pyramid construction. | Old Kingdom Pyramid Age (3675–2600 BC). | Great Pyramid evidence. | Engineer’s village. |
| Djoser = name = true pyramids. | Djoser (3675–3646 BC) → Khufu (3460–3397 BC). | Djoser Step Pyramid, Giza. | Pyramid evolution. |
| 2nd oldest boat. | Khufu’s boat (3460–3397 BC). | Khufu’s solar boat. | Riverine technology. |
| Surgical tools. | Proto-Unification (before 4500 BC) → Early Dynastic. | Tomb finds. | Surgical innovation. |
| Bone, bronze, iron forging (5000–1000 BC). | Proto-Unification (before 4500 BC). | Tool remains. | Foundation of metal industry. |
| First dental surgery. | Middle Kingdom → New Kingdom III–Akhenaton, 1412–1293 BC. | Amarna workshops. | Health advancement. |
| Mummification. | Old Kingdom II–III. | Embalming records. | Burial science. |
| Alchemy. | Middle Kingdom, 12th Dynasty (Senwosret III, 2112–2074 BC). | Rhind & Moscow Papyri. | Chemistry. |
| 365-day calendar. | Late Period, 30th Dynasty (360–343 BC). | Saqqara aldiar calendar. | Global calendar basis. |
