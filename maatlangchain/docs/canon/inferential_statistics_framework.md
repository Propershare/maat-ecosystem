Title: Inferential Statistics Framework

| Key Concept / Definition | Formula / Expression | When to Use | How to Use | Why to Use (Purpose) | Step-by-Step Process | SPSS Menu |
|--------------------------|-----------------------|-------------|-------------|------------------------|------------------------|-------------|
| Tests whether a sample mean differs from a population mean when σ is known. | Z = (X̄ - μ) / (σ / √N) | Large sample (N > 30), known σ | Compute Z, compare to ±1.96 at α = 0.05. | Tests deviation from known population mean. | 1. Define H₀. 2. Compute Z. 3. Compare Z to critical Z. 4. Interpret. | Analyze → Compare Means → One-Sample T-Test (approximation). |
| Tests whether sample mean differs from a population mean when σ unknown. | t = (X̄ - μ) / (s / √N) | Small sample (N ≤ 30), σ unknown. | Compute sample SD; find t, df = N - 1. | Compares sample to known constant. | Same as Z but uses t. | Analyze → Compare Means → One-Sample T-Test. |
| Compares means between two independent groups. | t = (X̄1 - X̄2)/(√(s²₁/n₁ + s²₂/n₂)) | Two groups, interval/ratio data. | Check equality of variances (Levene’s test). | Detects mean difference between groups. | 1. H₀: μ₁ = μ₂. 2. Compute t. 3. Compare with t-critical. | Analyze → Compare Means → Independent-Samples T-Test. |
| Compares means of same group at two times or matched pairs. | t = ΣD / (√(ΣD² - (ΣD)² / n) / (n-1)) | Pre-post or matched design. | Compute difference scores. | Tests within-subject change. | 1. H₀: D = 0. 2. Compute mean diff. 3. Compare with critical t. | Analyze → Compare Means → Paired-Samples T-Test. |
| Compares means of 3+ groups. | F = MSB / MSW | IV categorical (≥3 levels), DV continuous. | Compute between- & within-group variance. | Detects mean difference among groups. | 1. H₀: μ₁ = μ₂ = μ₃. 2. Compute MS. 3. Get F, check p. | Analyze → General Linear Model → Univariate ANOVA. |
| Tests effects of two IVs and interaction. | F = MSfactor / MSerror | Two factors, one continuous DV. | Compute main and interaction effects. | Checks interaction difference between IVs. | Same as above, add second IV. | Analyze → General Linear Model → Univariate ANOVA. |
| Tests multiple DVs across groups. | Wilks’ Λ, Pillai’s Trace | ≥2 DVs, 1+ categorical IV. | Assess combined mean vectors. | Detects group effects. | Multivariate testing. | Analyze → General Linear Model → Multivariate. |
| Adjusts DV for effects of covariates. | F = MSadjusted / MSerror | DV continuous, IV categorical, covariate interval. | Remove covariate influence. | Controls for third variable. | Same as ANOVA, with covariates. | Analyze → General Linear Model → Univariate → Add covariates. |
| Tests mean differences across ≥3 time points or conditions. | F = MSwithin / MSerror | Same subjects across time. | Use same subjects. Analyze t
