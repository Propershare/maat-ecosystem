## 1. Geographic Division of Kmt

Kmt is organized into three major regions aligned along the Nile River:

- **Lower African Kmt**: Northern region of Kmt, closer to the Nile Delta and Mediterranean Sea.

- **Upper African Kmt**: Southern Kmt, upstream on the Nile River, often considered the origin point of dynastic unification.

These divisions reflect both geographical and administrative realities in ancient times.
