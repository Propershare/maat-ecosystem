# Kmtian Foundation and Modern Meritocracy

## Modern Expression
- **Scientific federal state-planning**: empirical planning.
- **Party schools and universities**: education structured as state training systems.
- **Seba and engineers**: knowledge-based bureaucracy.
- **Politburo, State Council**: centralized policy coordination.
- **Lifelong Seba evaluation**: adaptive and self-correcting meritocracy.

## Comparison: Ancient Kemet (c. 4500 BCE – 300 BCE) vs. Modern State Meritocracy (CCP-era China)

### Ancient Kemet
- **Maat Principle**: Truth, balance, and justice as cosmic law; state governed by moral-scientific precision and the doctrine of opposites.
- **Per-Ankh (House of Life)**: Temple-academies training scribes, physicians, engineers, astronomers in math, medicine, and ethics.
- **Curriculum**: Logic, geometry, astronomy, hydraulics, agriculture, KA and Maat laws; emphasized record-keeping (Sesh Medew Neter).
- **Evaluation**: Writing trials, problem-solving, administrative simulations.
- **Post-graduation**: Service in local offices, temples, and construction under senior officials; merit judged by balance and efficiency.
- **Per-Medjat (House of Records)**: Data collection on labor, grain, taxation, and public works.
- **Ethical training**: KA (spirit) seen as inseparable from technical skill.
- **Final judgment**: Maat feather test in afterlife symbolizes performance review.

### Modern State Meritocracy (CCP-era China)
- **Principle of Scientific Socialism**: Dialectical materialism as law of social order; empirical governance.
- **Schools/Universities**: Institutions like Tsinghua and the Central Party School train cadre in policy, administration, and science.
- **Curriculum**: Marxist theory, data analysis, planning, evidence-based governance.
- **Evaluation**: Exams, performance metrics, policy simulations.
- **Cadre practice**: Local governance experience in towns and counties.
- **Monitoring**: National Bureau of Statistics, NDRC use data on population, production, and education.
- **Ethical training**: Officials judged on public service ethics and efficiency.
- **Discipline**: Party inspections, self-criticism sessions.

## Core Scientific Continuities
- Unified moral-scientific law governs both.
- Rational planning and merit-based governance.
- Objective evaluation systems integrated with ethical conduct.

> This comparison shows the continuity of scientific meritocratic principles from ancient Kemet to modern models like that of the CCP's governance structure.
