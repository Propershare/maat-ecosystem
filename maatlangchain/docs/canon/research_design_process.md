**Research Design and Statistical Analysis Process**

1. **Identify the Research Problem**
   - Define whether the purpose is to explain processes, behaviors, etc., or to test specific hypotheses regarding possible causal relationships.
   - Select method: **Descriptive** or **Experimental**.

2. **Literature Review**
   - Review existing literature to clearly define the research problem.

3. **Draft the Problem Statement**
   - Write a precise statement of the problem and context.

4. **Construct a Plan of Action**
   - Consider accessibility of population.
   - Procedures for selecting participants.
   - Define data collection design and methods.
   - Identify resource needs and availability.
   - Timeline planning.

5. **Conduct a Comprehensive Literature Review**
   - Evaluate and address critical key issues.
   - Note research gaps.
   - Build rationale for study.

6. **Theoretical Framework / Hypothesis Testing**
   - Define key research questions.
   - Specify hypothesis grounded in theory.
   - Determine independent and dependent variables.

7. **Select Appropriate Statistical Test & Analysis Design**
   - Consider:
     - Level of measurement (nominal, ordinal, interval, ratio).
     - Number of variables.
     - Descriptive vs. inferential methods.
     - Statistical procedures like ANOVA, regression, correlation, chi-square.

8. **Select Data Collection Design**
   - Choose between **Experimental**, **Quasi-Experimental**, or **Non-Random Experimental** designs.
   - Random assignment and group selection as needed.

9. **Instrument Selection**
   - Use existing instruments or develop new ones.
   - Construct or adapt data collection tools.

10. **Data Collection & Preparation**
    - Gather data according to design.
    - Clean raw data: remove errors, handle missing values.

11. **Organize & Analyze Data**
    - Decide if data should be condensed or simplified.
    - Create frequency distributions, visual representations.
    - Compute descriptive stats: frequency, central tendency, variability.
    - Identify patterns: correlations, trends, relationships.

12. **Interpret Results**
    - Establish statistical significance.
    - Explore relationships.
    - Evaluate hypothesis outcomes.

13. **Draw Conclusions & Report**
    - Summarize findings.
    - Link to hypotheses and literature.
    - Present implications, limitations, and suggestions for future research.
