# Evidence Analysis Model

## SOURCES
*Documents, registers, publications, artifacts, people, websites, etc.*
- **Original Record**
- **Derivative Record**
- **Authored Narrative**

## INFORMATION
*Judged by informant's level of participation or knowledge*
- **Primary (firsthand)**
- **Secondary (secondhand)**
- **Undetermined**

## EVIDENCE
*Based on relevance of information to question and degree of explicitness*
- **Direct (relevant / explicit)**
- **Indirect (relevant / not explicit)**
- **Negative (relevant / not explicit)**

---

## ANALYSIS → "PROOF"
*Proof is derived through critical analysis of the relationship between source, information, and evidence.*
