# Critical Inferences and What Must Be Done

| **Critical Inferences** | **What Must Be Done** |
|--------------------------|------------------------|
| Intellectuals relied on external funding, beholden and unable to act independently. | **Break dependency**: Build self-funded research centers. Control the means. |
| Decades of rhetoric yielded no victories. | **Build real institutions**: Channel into cooperatives, and cultural centers. |
| Leaders compromised for grants. | **Reject careerism**: Train leaders for liberation. Liberation requires uncompromising courage. |
| African-centered scholarship was watered down, made “safe.” | **Reclaim African knowledge**: Study without needing Western approval. Anchor education in African frameworks. |
| Black intellectual culture imitated others, losing African authenticity. | **Achieve cultural independence**: Dismantle myths. Ground identity and morals in African truth. |
| Intellectuals stayed in symbolic debates and theory. | **Make science central**: Train scientists, build infrastructure for African independence and progress. |
| Redirected Black anger into “safe” zones—no real threat to the system. | **Refuse containment**: Transform rhetoric into institutions. Replace symbolic acts with action. |
| Intellectuals conformed for safety, leaving no legacy. | **Practice courage**: Prepare youth for sacrifice and progress. Real leadership requires risk. |
| Intellectuals were insulated from the masses. | **Unite with working class**: Ground theory in community research, training, and resources. |
| Universities produced degrees but not leaders. | **Train youth for independence**: End dependency. Teach practical skills, science, and leadership. |
