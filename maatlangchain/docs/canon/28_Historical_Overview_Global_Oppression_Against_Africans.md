# Historical Overview of Global Oppression Against Africans

This chart outlines a historical overview of global oppression against Africans, from ancient times (e.g., the Hyksos invasion) to the modern day. It details the nature of each oppression, its impact on Africanist culture, and the long-term consequences.

## Key Sections

| Event/Invader | Nature of Oppression | Impact on Africanist | Legacy/Consequences |
|---------------|---------------------|---------------------|---------------------|
| Hyksos Invasion (Northern) | Military invasion, foreign rule | Disrupted unity in Lower Kemet | Set precedent for future invasions |
| Assyrian Conquest | Brutal military attacks | Temple destruction, deaths | Weakened native dynasties |
| Persian Invasion | Bureaucracy, taxation | Africans treated as subjects | Decline of indigenous priesthoods |
| Greek Invasion (Alexander) | Hellenization, cultural erasure | Temples defiled, Greek language | Theft of knowledge (e.g., Library of Alexandria) |
| Roman Colonization | Exploitation, corruption | African gods demonized | Romanization of Egypt, loss of African face |
| Christianization (Byzantine) | Religious conversion | Suppressed traditions | Entrenched foreign religions |
| Islamic Invasion | Conquest, Islamic imperialism | Islamized Black populations | Arabization of Kemet and Nile Valley |
| Arab Slave Raids | Capture/enslavement | Growth of Trans-Saharan slave trade | Basis for racialized slavery |
| European Slave Trade | Abduction, genocide | 125M Africans displaced/destroyed | Psychological trauma, racism |
| Napoleonic Invasion of Egypt | Military + academic invasion | Looting of monuments | Birth of Eurocentric Egyptology |
| Berlin Conference (1884) | Africa partitioned without Africans | Loss of sovereignty | Formal colonial rule |
| European Colonial Rule | Enforced racism | Enforced European education | Suppressed native culture/language |
| Apartheid South Africa | Legal racial segregation | Land/labor theft | Mass incarceration, inequality |
| Globalization/Structural Adjustment | Economic control via debt/NGOs | Controlled resources | Ongoing poverty |
| Modern Denial of African Roots | Denial of African Nile Valley origins | Cultural erasure | Ethnic cleansing, anti-Black policies |
| Appropriation of African History | False claims to African legacy | Cultural theft | Global ignorance of African origins |

## Summary

The chart emphasizes how foreign invasions, colonization, and cultural domination across centuries:

- Systematically dismantled African civilizations
- Rewrote history to suit foreign powers
- Enforced cultural erasure, slavery, economic exploitation, and racialized oppression
- Led to modern structural inequality, poverty, and global misrepresentation of African contributions to civilization

It's a strong critique of both historical and ongoing global systems that undermine African autonomy and legacy.
