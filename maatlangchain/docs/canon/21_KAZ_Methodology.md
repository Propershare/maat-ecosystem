# KAZ Methodology

## Mode of Appropriation

1. **Identify the research problem** – establish time dimension  
2. **Conduct comprehensive literature review** – include prior scientific and historical research  
3. **Select data & sources** – documents, empirical data, diagrams, oral histories, etc.  
4. **Identify process & procedure** – define how data will be analyzed  
5. **Secure/gather data** – from primary and secondary sources  
6. **Critically validate data** – review for race, class, culture, generational bias  
7. **Organize data** – notebooks, standardized documentation

---
