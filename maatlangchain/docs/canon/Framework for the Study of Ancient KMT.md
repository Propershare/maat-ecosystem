## Framework for the Study of Ancient KMT

| Ancient Kmtian Term (Transliteration) | Meaning / Role in Kmtian Thought | Purpose | Key Activities | Risks if Omitted |
|--------------------------------------|------------------------------------|---------|----------------|------------------|
| **Per-Sesh** (House of Sesh/Accounting) | The place where problems and questions are formulated | Define the central research question about Kmt’s birth, development, decline, and death | - State the historical problem clearly (e.g., causes of collapse of Old Kingdom)  
- Identify scope (chronological & thematic) | Research drifts into speculation without clear focus |
| **Medew Neter** | Refers to sacred inscriptions, texts, and records | Survey prior interpretations, both ancient and modern | - Review classical sources (Herodotus, Manetho)  
- Review modern archaeology, African-centered revisions (Finch, Diop) | Risk of recycling old Eurocentric biases or myths |
| **Ma’at** (Truth, Justice, Balance, Order) | Guiding principle of justice and cosmic order | Ground research in a clear interpretive framework | - Apply birth-death-rebirth (KA Method)  
- Integrate environmental, social, and political lenses | Risk of fragmented or biased conclusions without coherent theory |
| **Sesh** (Writing, Recording) | The act of designing and recording knowledge | Plan systematic steps to investigate Kmt’s life cycle | - Define chronological units (Early Dynastic → Late Period)  
- Define institutions (state, economy, religion) | Research becomes piecemeal, not replicable |
| **Per-Ankh** (House of Life) | Temple school for knowledge, medicine, and archives | Establish methodological tools for inquiry | - Archaeology  
- Textual criticism  
- Comparative anthropology | Using only one method creates one-dimensional history |
| **Khu** (The Thing, Artifact) | Refers to material reality, artifacts | Identify categories of relevant evidence | - Artifacts, tombs, pyramids  
- Texts (Pyramid Texts, Coffin Texts)  
- Environmental records (Nile flood layers) | Risk of missing whole dimensions of history |
| **Seba** (To Instruct, Teach) | Kmtian concept of ordering/arranging things properly | Organize evidence into coherent categories | - Stratigraphy of artifacts  
- Thematic ordering by institution | Chaos of data, inability to trace cause-effect |
| **Duat** (Afterlife Realm) | Term used for verified truth, often in afterlife context | Ensure authenticity of evidence | - Verify provenance  
- Eliminate forgeries | Use of dynastic timelines  
Reliance on propaganda texts |
| **Khunu** (Knowledge, Insight) | Kmtian word for deep knowledge or understanding | Examine evidence comparatively | - Comparative chronology (Mesopotamia, Mali, Hittites) | Oversimplified causes (e.g., invasion theory) |
| **Smai Tawi** (Unity of Two Lands) | Symbol of integration, unity | Build coherent historical narrative | - Construct unified timeline  
- Link multiple causes (environment + politics) | Fragmented dynastic histories  
Overemphasis on elites (e.g., “Kmt collapsed with the Sea Peoples”) |
| **Nebu** (Gold) | The binding principle of vitality & interconnection | Demonstrate how synthesis works | - Verification grounds analysis  
- Analysis reveals causes  
- Synthesis creates meaning | Without verification: myths & propaganda dominate  
Without synthesis: disjointed explanations, disconnected “facts” |

This framework ensures an accurate, balanced, and systematic reconstruction of Kmtian civilization.
