# Research Design and Statistical Analysis Process

---

## 1. Identify the Research Problem
- Purpose: Explain, predict, or test hypotheses
- Define specific hypotheses or research questions

---

## 2. Literature Review
- Review to define the research problem clearly
- Draft a precise statement of the problem and its context

---

## 3. Plan of Action
- Assess accessibility of population
- Determine participant selection methods
- Choose data collection design, data analysis design
- Determine resources needed and timeframe

---

## 4. Project Flowchart
- Evaluate scope of research
- Define sampling strategy: 
  - Simple Random
  - Systematic
  - Stratified Random
  - Cluster
- Establish topic rationale and significance
- Define operational terms
- Establish tools for measurement and data collection

---

## 5. Conduct Comprehensive Literature Review
- Critically analyze previous research
- Create Theoretical Framework/Conceptual Model
- Link research questions to theory
- Develop hypothesis

---

## 6. Select Data Collection Design

### Experimental Design?
- Yes:
  - Random Assignment
    - Pretest-Posttest Control Group
    - Posttest Only Control Group
    - Solomon Four Group
- No:
  - Quasi-Experimental
    - Non-Random
      - Pretest-Posttest
      - Posttest Only
      - Time Series
      - Control Time Series
      - Separate Sample Pretest-Posttest
      - Equivalent Time Samples

---

## 7. Instrumentation
- Use existing instrument?
  - Yes: Use
  - No: Develop new instrument
- Construct data collection instrument
  - Interviews
  - Observations
  - Surveys
  - Likert Scales
- Secure code book and process for coding responses

---

## 8. Data Collection
- Gather data using selected design

---

## 9. Data Processing
- Organize and process raw data
- Clean errors, biases, etc.
- Determine whether to condense or simplify data
- Create frequency distribution and histogram if needed

---

## 10. Compute Measures

### Frequency
- Present relative frequency of categories

### Central Tendency
- Mean, Median, Mode

### Variability
- Range, Variance, Standard Deviation

### Association
- Cross-tabulation, Chi-square

### Linear Relationship
- Pearson correlation, Regression

---

## 11. Statistical Design Selection

### Consider:
- Level of measurement (nominal, ordinal, interval, ratio)
- Number of variables: Independent/Dependent
- Statistical purpose:
  - Descriptive
  - Associational
  - Inferential

---

## 12. Determine Statistical Tests
- Parametric or Non-Parametric
- Examples:
  - t-tests
  - ANOVA
  - Correlation
  - Regression
  - Chi-square
  - Mann-Whitney U

---

## 13. Establish Purpose of Statistical Analysis
- Test hypotheses
- Explore relationships
- Estimate parameters
- Explain interactions or effects
