# Modernized African KMT Calendar

## Foundational Global Calendar
- Kmt (Ancient Egypt) created the modern 365-day calendar:
  - 12 months of 30 days
  - 5 or 6 epagomenal days added
- Time division:
  - Day: 24 hours (12 by day, 12 by night)
  - Hour further subdivided into smaller units
- Kmt had three calendar systems:
  - Lunar
  - Civil
  - Astronomical
- The civil calendar was based on the heliacal rising of Sirius (Sopdet), aligned with the flooding of the Nile

## Cycle
- **Sopdet (Sirius)** heliacal rising occurred in **1461-year cycles**
- Identified key years initiating Kmt's civilized history:
  - **7163 BC**
  - **5702 BC**
  - **4241 BC**
  - **2780 BC**
  - **1319 BC**
  - **142 BC**
