# RBG Library Overview & Priority Map

The RBG Library folder holds hundreds of radical, Afrocentric, and counterhegemonic texts—autobiographies, political economics, strategic plans, scientific reconstructions, spiritual treatises, and movement archives. The corpus supplies the non-peer-reviewed pulse behind the Tehuti canon, so I’m mapping the highlights here to guide TRA’s next ingestion wave.

## 1. African History & Civilizational Origins
- **Cheikh Anta Diop (Diop-centric files):** `cheikh_anta_diop_civilization_of_barbarism.txt`, `cheikh_anta_diop_precolonial_black_africa.txt`, `cheikh_anta_diop~_cultural_unity_of_black_africa.txt` — foundational work on African scientific chronology and cultural unity.
- **Ngugi wa Thiong'o & Shola:** `ngugi's_novels_and_african_history.txt`, `ngugi_wa_thiongo_moving_the_centre(1).txt`, etc. — language, ideological liberation, and narrative politics.
