|------------------------|------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------|
| Communalism/-x         | Kin-based subsistence, shared means      | Low surplus vs. growing needs                       |                                                  | Emergence of surplus society                     |
| Master-slave           | Master-slave, expansion by conquest      | Limits of extensive growth; revolts; costs of empire| Attempts to intensify extraction                 | Disintegration of empires                        |
| Lord-serf              | Lord-serf, land rent, Church hegemony    | Peasant resistance; town/market revival             | Feudal-lords revive slave-like relations in periphery | Proto-bourgeois forms                           |
| Proto-colonial         | Merchant capital, protected trade        | Need for massive cheap labor & raw inputs           | Transatlantic chattel slavery modeled on Rome    | Primitive accumulation; enclosure                |
| Capitalism             | Wage labor, factory system               | Overproduction vs. limited wages/markets            | Coercive labor regimes in colonies               | Global expansion of capital                      |
| Late capitalism         | Integration of mining, agri, construction, services | Falling wage share; crises; imperial rivalry   | Financialization, super-exploitation in periphery | Monopoly & tech sector dominance                 |
| Robotics               | Capital substitutes labor at scale       | Shrinking consumer base; profit-demand gaps         | Precarity, platformization, surveillance-labor    | Structural crisis                                |
