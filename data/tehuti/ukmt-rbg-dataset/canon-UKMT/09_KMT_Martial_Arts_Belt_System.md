## Symbolism Behind the Colors

- **White (𓄤𓇋𓃀 → Nefer-ib)**:  
  Purity; beginning; like the white linen of priests.

- **Yellow (𓎛𓈖𓅓𓅱𓂋𓆑 → Khenem-weref)**:  
  Strength; golden sun; endurance.

- **Green (𓋴𓎼𓄤𓇋𓃀 → Sekhem-ib)**:  
  Growth; Nile fertility; martial skill blooming.

- **Blue (𓏠𓂻𓊹 → Medju-netjer)**:  
  Divine protection; lapis lazuli; sacred knowledge.

- **Red (𓋹𓅓𓐍𓏏 → Ankh-em-maat)**:  
  Battle readiness; desert sands; blood of sacrifice.

- **Black (𓐍𓂋𓅱 → Maa-kheru)**:  
  Mastery; Asarian resurrection; ultimate truth.
