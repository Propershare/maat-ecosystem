# Tehuti Research Agent (TRA)

**Objective:** Build an investigator that out-thinks any single PhD on African-centered methodology by embedding the 25 canonical research artifacts from the Tehuti canon into a single, Maat-guided cognitive stack.

## 1. Mission Pillars (sources in parentheses)
- **Systemic mapping:** TRA models modern contexts with the *United States Societal Anatomy* (01) plus the capitalism development timelines (18, 15, 24) to understand the ecological, financial, and ideological terrain of any research question.
- **Design discipline:** The agent checks every design decision against *Fundamental Research Design Errors* (02) and the *Research Design and Statistical Analysis Process* (04) before formalizing hypotheses.
- **Statistical mastery:** *Statistical Decision Tree and Measures* (03) acts as the decision engine for test selection, ensuring parametric/non-parametric choices match measurement levels, numbers of variables, and inferential goals.
- **Maat/KMT epistemology:** TRA codifies the *MAAT Codes* (10) and *KMT research process tables* (11, 14, 20) so every recommendation stays morally sound, balanced, and traceable to ancient African disciplines.
- **KAZ dialectical methodology:** *KAZ Methodology* (21) becomes the meta-process: identify time-anchored problems, validate sources, locate contradictions, track nodal transition points, and narrate negation/reversal cycles.
- **Diop perspective:** *Diop Framework* (22) keeps the agent sensitive to framing harms and ensures findings re-center Black genius rather than repeat Eurocentric myths.

## 2. Workflow Stack
1. **Problem Contextualization (01, 23):** Parse textual data, timelines, and systemic anatomy to define nodes (economy, ideology, social) and their contradictions. Include US state/regional variations and KMT chronology comparisons.
2. **Significance & Literature (16, multiple canon files):** Use Table of Contents documents (16) and relevant histories to map prior knowledge, building a chronology of ideas so the agent sees where gaps remain.
3. **Hypothesis & Design (04, 11, 14, 20):** Translate node-level contradictions into hypotheses, choose a path (experimental/quasi/mixed), design instrumentation, define coding protocols, and adopt KMT terminology for clarity.
4. **Data Curation (05–09, 17, 19, 25):** Pull from cultural, scientific, and economic sources (UKMT, canonical KMT inventions, state-planned economy notes) to gather fact sets; integrate AI/technology risk data (13) for modern relevance.
5. **Statistical & Analytical Execution (03):** The agent consults the decision tree for each variable combination and uses the right test suite (chi-square, ANOVA, regression, etc.), adjusting for measurement level and sample structure.
6. **Ethics & Narrative (10, 22, 25):** Deploy Maat codes and Diop safeguards—avoid moral vanity, keep modesty, and ensure accountability; frame results as restorative, not punitive.
7. **Interpretation Layer:** The agent layers findings on the KAZ life-cycle (periods, transitions, regressions) and the socio-historical timeline to link evidence back to mission goals.
8. **Action Loop:** It turns insights into research briefs, strategic recommendations (including resource allocation, team formation, or policy pushes), and simulation-ready outputs (graphs, matrices, event-driven models from 21).

## 3. Intelligence & Learning
- **Signal fidelity:** Continuous validation against *Fundamental Research Design Errors* and KAZ data validation steps ensures bias checks.
- **Meta-reflection:** Every deliverable gets a Maat scorecard (truth, order, balance, justice, self-reflection) plus a regression watch (RBL) and negation-of-negation tracking.
- **Historical anchoring:** Chronology doc (23) ensures that new claims are properly dated, and the *Comparison of Oppression* (24) keeps the geopolitical stakes clear.

## 4. Operational Components
- **Data connectors:** Link to the UKMT corpus and the Tehuti dataset; ingest structured tables, oral histories, timelines, and socio-technological intelligence.
- **Analytical tools:** Build reusable components for distribution tables, frequency histograms, and correlation matrices (03); manage instrumentation, coding guides, and flowcharts (14, 20).
- **Evaluation metrics:** Monitor precision, sampling bias, instrumentation reliability, and hypothesis coverage; feed these into dashboards that compare outcomes to the original KAZ life-cycle.

## 5. Guardrails & Growth
- **Ethical guardrails:** Use MAAT codes (10) and Diop framework (22) to keep research restorative. Flag any recommendation that risks repeating colonial narratives.
- **Mission feedback:** Track how each initiative serves the goal of fueling the Maat project and the UKMT intelligence pipeline; adjust priorities when contradictions (KAZ) or regression signals (RBL) appear.
- **100x PhD edge:** The combination of systemic mapping, statistical rigor, dialectical time-tracking, and Maat-aligned ethics plus automated data ingestion/interpretation ensures the TRA outpaces siloed academic expertise.
