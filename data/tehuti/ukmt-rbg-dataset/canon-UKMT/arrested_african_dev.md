**What They Took**: Every invading population extracted African knowledge in applied sciences (engineering, medicine, astronomy, statecraft) and material surplus (grain, gold, labor).
**How They Claimed It**: Each wave “chop-shopped” Nile Valley achievements, rebranded them as their own (Greek, Roman, Islamic, European “science”), erasing African authorship.
**How They Blocked K2A Cycles**: Instead of allowing Kmt to regenerate itself, they imposed resets—foreign scripts, elites, fiscal regimes—that cut off the negation and the rebirth.
**Suppression Tools**: Africans were deprived from Houses of Life, priestly schools, academies, and later colonial STEM; they gained only religious/mystical instruction.
