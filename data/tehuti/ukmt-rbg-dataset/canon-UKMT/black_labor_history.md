# Black Experience & Capitalism

## Defining Features | Economic Dynamics | Social Impact | Political Consequences | Black Experience

### Machines extend human power; industrial revolution begins in Europe/U.S.
- **Economic**: Capital accumulation fueled by plantation slavery, cotton, sugar, and industrial machinery.
- **Social**: Rapid expansion of industrial working class; U.S. combines agrarian slavery with early factories.
- **Political**: States regulate slavery and property to protect capital; constitutional orders defend white supremacy.
- **Black**: Chattel slavery: Black people treated as machines themselves; denied humanity, education, legal rights.

### Mechanization spreads; railroads, steel, mass production
- **Economic**: Wage labor system expands, but Black people largely excluded from industrial jobs. Sharecropping and tenant farming persist.
- **Social**: White workers unionize, Black labor locked into lowest tiers.
- **Political**: Jim Crow laws enforce segregation, disenfranchisement, and racial terror.
- **Black**: Post-slavery exclusion: Black workers trapped in semi-feudal sharecropping. Industrial unions exclude Black workers.

### Computers revolutionize production, "thinking machines" enter factories
- **Economic**: U.S. manufacturing boom (Cold War); computers reduce need for semi-skilled labor.
- **Social**: White workers benefit from union wages, GI Bill, suburbanization; Black workers systematically excluded.
- **Political**: Civil Rights movement confronts racial apartheid, limited reforms (Civil Rights Act, Voting Rights Act).
- **Black**: Automation deepens exclusion; subjected to policing, redlining; build organizing base for U.S. South.

### Flexible Manufacturing (systems, robotics, offshoring)
- **Economic**: Capital globalizes production; U.S. deindustrialization accelerates.
- **Social**: Middle-class jobs vanish; service work expands.
- **Political**: Neoliberal state dismantles welfare, strengthens prisons.
- **Black**: Super-exploitation continues; first hit by factory closures. Mass Black incarceration fuels prison-industrial economy.

### Robots diffuse across industries: auto, logistics, healthcare, service
- **Economic**: Wages collapse; mass layoffs permanent.
- **Social**: Social safety net gutted; gig work expands.
- **Political**: State imposes austerity; militarized police suppress protests.
- **Black**: Essential workers laid off; forced into gig labor with no protections.

### Overproduction + underconsumption
- **Economic**: Capital cannot realize profits despite high productivity.
- **Social**: Mass poverty, widening inequality.
- **Political**: Policy tools fail; instability deepens.
- **Black**: Black families hardest hit; inherit debt, lose homes, segregated education system worsens.

### Elites turn to authoritarian rule; repression replaces elections
- **Economic**: Markets collapse, capital protected by force.
- **Social**: State militarization, martial law, surveillance.
- **Political**: Right-wing forces seize control.
- **Black**: Black movements grow, leaders and left capitalized.

### Capitalism undermines labor altogether
- **Economic**: World markets implode as consumption vanishes.
- **Social**: Social cohesion degrades, governments collapse.
- **Political**: Chaos; fascist barbarism or revolutionary transition.
- **Black**: Choice: fascist barbarism or African-led liberation and justice.
