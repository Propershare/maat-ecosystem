# Birth, Development, Decline, and Decay of USA Capitalism

## Phases of Capital Accumulation

### Phase 3: Early Accumulation of Capital
- **Event:** Annihilation of Indigenous populations & social formations
- **Diagram Labels:** 
  - Triangle: `pr`, `pf`
  - Base: `C ⊗ sl`

---

### Phase 4: Enslavement & Manual Capital Formation
- **Event:** Harnessing of unpaid, free labor source
- **Diagram Labels:**
  - Triangle: `pr`, `pf`
  - Base: `C`

---

### Phase 5: Industrial Fusion
- **Event:** "Identity": unity of slave-manual & capitalist-mechanical production
- **Diagram Labels:**
  - Triangle: `pr`, `pf`, `sf`
  - Base: `C ⊗`

---

### Phase 6: Semi-Automation Crisis
- **Event:** Large-scale semi-automated mechanical production & global crisis of expansion
- **Diagram Labels:**
  - Triangle: `ir`, `sf`, `cp`, `cnf`
  - Base: `C`

---

### Phase 7: CAMP (Crisis of Advanced Machine Production)
- **Event:** CAMP in the context of the STR (Systemic Transition Regime)
- **Diagram Labels:**
  - Triangle: `ir`, `sf`, `cp`, `cnf`

---

### Phase 8: Regression & RBL
- **Event:** Entering the epoch of social counter-revolution (RBL)
  - Regression back to pre-capitalist social relations: feudal, slave
- **Diagram Labels:**
  - Triangle only (no base): regression phase

---

## Legend of Terms
- `pr`: Productive relations
- `pf`: Productive forces
- `sf`: Slave forces
- `cp`: Capitalist production
- `cnf`: Conflict
- `ir`: Industrial relations
- `C`: Capital
- `⊗`: Fusion of systems
- `sl`: Slavery
- `RBL`: Regression Back Line
- `STR`: Systemic Transition Regime
