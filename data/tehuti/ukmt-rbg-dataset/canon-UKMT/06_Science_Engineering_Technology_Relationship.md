# The Relationship Among Science, Engineering, and Technology

## Science
- Scientists seek to understand natural phenomena.
- Scientific knowledge often informs and helps engineers and technicians.

## Engineering
- Engineers use scientific principles to design products and processes that meet society's needs.

## Technology
- Technologies (products and processes) are the result of engineered designs.
- Created by technicians to solve societal needs and wants.

## Intersection
- The Venn diagram illustrates the overlap and interdependence of science, engineering, and technology.
- Each domain supports and enhances the others in developing solutions and advancing knowledge.
