### ARAB-SLAVE-TRADES-AND-AFRICAN-CHIEFS
#### Impact of the Trans-Saharan and Arab Slave Holocaust on Africans in Relation to African Chiefs (7th–19th centuries)

| Domestic workers, domestic slaves, agricultural workers | Plantation labor (sugar, cotton, tobacco), mines, maritime labor | Chiefs supplied captives from internal wars, raids, debt-bondage |
