# Stages of Capitalism, Labor & Black Experience

| Defining Features | Economic Dynamics | Social Impact | Political Consequences | Black Experience / Exploitation |
|------------------|------------------|---------------|--------------------------|----------------------------------|
| Machines extend human power; industrial revolution begins in Europe/U.S. | Capital accumulation fueled by plantation slavery, cotton, sugar, and industrial machinery. | Rapid expansion of industrial working class; U.S. combines agrarian slavery with early factories. | States regulate slavery and property to protect capital; constitutional orders defend white supremacy. | **Chattel slavery**: Black labor as machines themselves; no wages. U.S. industrial wealth derived from enslaved African labor; literacy and rights denied. |
| Mechanization spreads, railroads, steel, mass production. | Wage labor system expands, yet Black people largely excluded from industrial jobs. Sharecropping and tenant farming persist. | White workers unionize, Black labor locked into lowest tiers. | Jim Crow laws enforce segregation, disenfranchisement, and racial terror. | **Post-slavery exploitation**: Black labor confined to semi-feudal sharecropping and lowest wage sectors; excluded from unions; subjected to racial terrorism. |
| Computers revolutionize production, "thinking machines" enter factories. | U.S. manufacturing boom of Cold War investments; computers reduce need for semi-skilled labor. | White workers benefit from union wages, GI Bill, suburbanization; Black workers systematically excluded. | Civil Rights movement confronts racial apartheid; limited reforms (Civil Rights Act, Voting Rights Act). | Automation deepens exclusion: Black labor excluded from semi-skilled industrial jobs by racism + deindustrialization. Mass Black incarceration becomes labor base for U.S. South. |
| Flexible manufacturing systems, robotics, offshoring. | Capital globalizes production; U.S. deindustrialization accelerates. | Middle-class jobs vanish; service work expands. | Neoliberal state dismantles welfare, strengthens prisons. | **Super-exploitation**: Mass incarceration, gig economy, policing of surplus populations. |
| Robots diffuse across industries: auto, logistics, healthcare, service. | Wages collapse; mass layoffs permanent. | Social safety nets gutted; gig work expands. | State imposes austerity; militarized police suppress protests. | State discipline for displaced Black workers; informal + criminalized survival economies. |
| Overproduction + underconsumption: Goods flood market, but workers lack income. | Capital cannot realize profits despite high productivity. | Mass poverty, widening inequality. | Policy tools fail; instability heightens. | Systemic exclusion: Black communities bear brunt of austerity + dispossession. |
| Elites turn to authoritarian rule; repression replaces elections. | Markets collapse, capital protected by force. | The state militarizes, martial law, surveillance. | Total movements grow, liberals and left capitulate. | **Crisis state**: fascist backlash against Black organizing, mass criminalization. |
| Capitalism undermines itself; global labor rebellion. | World markets implode as consumption vanishes. | Social collapse, delegitimized governments. | Chaos; fascist barbarism or global transformation. | Black liberation struggles catalyze new revolutionary horizon. |
