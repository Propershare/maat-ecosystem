# Research Process in Ancient Kmt Terms

| Category of Research     | Hieroglyphic Representation       | Transliteration     | Definition & Description                                                                 |
|--------------------------|------------------------------------|---------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Historical Research**  | ![𓎼𓏏𓏤𓄿𓂋𓏤𓏪𓊃](hieroglyph)         | Ḥzꜥ n ḏr.t          | "Study of the past times" – Investigates past events, cultures, and civilizations using primary sources like inscriptions, artifacts, and texts. |
| **Experimental Research**| ![𓋴𓏤𓈖𓄿𓂋𓏤𓏪𓊃](hieroglyph)         | Šnꜥ nṯr              | "Divine testing/trial" – Involves controlled experiments to test hypotheses, often in sciences like medicine or physics. |
| **Theoretical Research** | ![𓂧𓏤𓌳𓏏𓏤𓏪𓊃](hieroglyph)         | Ḏḥw m ḫꜥ            | "Words of truth/order" – Develops models, frameworks, or principles without direct experimentation; relies on logic and reasoning. |
| **Descriptive Research** | ![𓌳𓏏𓏤𓄿𓏤𓏪𓊃](hieroglyph)         | Sḏꜥ nḥḥ             | "Recording observations" – Documents characteristics of a phenomenon without manipulating variables (e.g., surveys, case studies). |
| **Applied Research**     | ![𓂋𓏤𓌳𓏏𓏤𓂋𓏤𓏪𓊃](hieroglyph)       | Rḏi ḥqꜣt            | "Solving practical matters" – Focuses on solving real-world problems or leading to technological or medical advances. |
| **Comparative Research** | ![𓅓𓄿𓏤𓌳𓏏𓏤𓏪𓊃](hieroglyph)         | Mꜥꜥ nḥḥ             | "Measuring differences" – Analyzes similarities and differences between groups, cultures, or systems to draw conclusions. |
| **Qualitative Research** | ![𓎼𓂋𓏏𓏤𓏪𓊃](hieroglyph)           | Grṭ nḥḥ              | "Examining qualities" – Explores non-numerical data (e.g., interviews, texts) to understand behaviors, relationships, and experiences. |
| **Quantitative Research**| ![𓎼𓏏𓏤𓈖𓏏𓏤𓏪𓊃](hieroglyph)         | Ḥsb nḥḥ              | "Counting numbers" – Uses statistical and mathematical analysis to measure variables and interpret relationships. |
