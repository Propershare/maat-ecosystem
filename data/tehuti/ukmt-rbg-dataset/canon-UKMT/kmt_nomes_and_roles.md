**KMT Nomes and Administrative Structure**

| Region | KMT-Egyptian Name (Translit.) | Governor Role (HqA Hwt) | Key State-Funded Socialist Functions |
|--------|-------------------------------|--------------------------|-------------------------------------|
| Upper Egypt (southern Nile Valley) | Various: e.g., *Ta-Seti* (tA-sty), *Waset* (wAst), *Abydos* ('bdw) | Directly subordinated to the Vizier; collected surplus grain, livestock, labor quotas | Each *nome* contributed to central granaries (*pr-Sn*), managed irrigation (*hwt-itrw*), operated temple schools (*pr-nTr*), and ensured redistribution of bread (*t*, *hnq.t*). They supplied labor (*mSa*), maintained regiments (*mSa.t*), and provided boats for transport. |
| Lower Egypt (northern Nile Delta) | Various: e.g., *Hathor Nome* (hwt-Hr), *Heliopolis* (Iunu), *Sais* (S3w) | Also subordinated to the Vizier; managed marshland irrigation, fishing, papyrus and linen production | Lower *nomes* specialized in papyrus, fish, fowl, and linen. Redistribution flowed toward temples, palaces, workers' villages, and health institutions. Iunu cult centers (Iunu tA nTr) produced remedies for medicine. Provided transport hubs for Nile-Delta defense and expeditions. |
