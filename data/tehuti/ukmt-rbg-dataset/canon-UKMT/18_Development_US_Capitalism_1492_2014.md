# Development of United States Capitalism: 1492 to 2014+

## Timeline of Phases

### 1. Pre-1492
- Indigenous social formations before colonial incursion
- Labeled as the stage of **Regression**

---

### 2. 1492–1600: Initial Societal Transplantation
- Emergence of slavery system
- Development of **transplanted society**

---

### 3. 1607–1609: Foundation
- Slavery in the South; capitalism emerges in the North
- Beginning of African Labor Force exploitation

---

### 4. 1619–1700: Embryonic Development of Dual Economic System
- Dual system:
  - Capitalism (North)
  - Slavery (South)

---

### 5. 1700–1749: Colonial to Embryonic National Formation
- African labor as base of development
- Capital and slave system forming national basis

---

### 6. 1750–1775: Transition Toward Nationalism
- Polarization of economic functions
- Laying groundwork for formal U.S. birth

---

### 7. 1776–1790: Formal Severance/Birth of USA
- Constitution formed
- Capitalism foundationally embedded in national structure

---

### 8. 1791–1820: Adolescent National Growth
- Capitalist consolidation
- African labor continues as economic base

---

### 9. 1856–1866: Large Scale Machine Production
- Resolution of Civil War conflict between capitalist North vs. slave South
- **Crisis** and transition phase

---

### 10. 1867–1920: Expansion
- Industrial maturity begins
- Socioeconomic shift from slave to wage labor

---

### 11. 1921–1949: Semi-Automated Machine Production
- Reconstruction along capitalist lines
- Economic modernization accelerates

---

### 12. 1950–1975: Automated Machine Production
- Mass production systems, consumer capitalism
- Full-scale **socio-economic maturation**

---

### 13. 1976–1990: Late Maturation Phase
- Automation and computerized production
- Beginning of **technological shift**

---

### 14. 1990–2014+: Computer-Automated Machine Production
- **Global Late Maturation**
- Rise of:
  - Productive Forces (Labor, Capital)
  - Reproductive Systems
  - Superstructural Institutions
  - Ideological Reordering

---

## Key Concepts
- **C**: Capital
- **S**: Slavery
- **SS**: Superstructure
- **IR**: Ideological Reordering
- **Basis**: Socioeconomic foundation
- **CAMP**: Computer-Automated Machine Production
- **STR**: Systemic Technological Reordering

---

## Historical Themes
- **Regression → Development → Crisis → Maturation → Decay → Rebirth**
- Societal organization determined by technology and labor systems
- U.S. capitalism built fundamentally on African labor and industrial transformation
