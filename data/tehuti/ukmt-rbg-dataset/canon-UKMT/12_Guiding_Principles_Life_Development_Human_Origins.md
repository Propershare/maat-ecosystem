# Guiding Principles of Life Development and Human Origins

## Guiding Principle #1:
- Sciences agree the Earth once existed in a state where no human life could exist.
- Over millions of years, life evolved from a single cell → animal life.
  - *Inorganic life to organic life*

---

## Guiding Principle #2:
- Step-by-step, increasingly complex life began to form.
  - From complex organic compounds to complex life
- Applies to Earth, ocean, and atmosphere.

---

## Guiding Principle #3:
- Simple cells synthesized with each other → more complex cells.
  - Formation of single-cell nucleus (well-defined)
  - *Foundation for different species*

---

## Guiding Principle #4:
- Single cell → multiple cells
- Everything has a unique time span; matter is in constant motion.
- Some species offer a general picture of life.

---

## Guiding Principle #5:
- Accelerated development: life moved from primitive pre-cellular → multicellular organisms.
  - Precellular → Cell organization → Multicellular organisms → Invertebrate → Vertebrate
- From mammals to primates → foundation for Homo Sapien Sapien (US)

---

## Guiding Principle #6:
- Primates were the evolutionary pinnacle of organic nature and the starting point of:
  - Origin, formation, phenotypic adaptation, differentiation of hominid life forms.
  - *Primates served as the building blocks for all human life forms*
  - "...everything is in motion and takes (own unique) time."

---

## Guiding Principle #7:
- The complete fossil record documenting five stages of hominid formation is found **exclusively in Africa**.

---

## Five Specimen All from Africa:
1. **Australopithecines**
2. **Homo habilis** *(tool users)*
3. **Homo erectus** *(first to emigrate out of Africa)*
4. **Homo sapien Neanderthal**
5. **Homo sapien sapien** *(existing specimen – currently)*

---

## Three Primary Routes Out of Africa:
1. **The Isthmus of Juez** *(the main route)*
2. **The northeast-south axis** (Kenya, Tanzania, Ethiopia → south Yemen)
3. **The northwest route**
