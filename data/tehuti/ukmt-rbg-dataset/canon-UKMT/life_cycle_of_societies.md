**Historical Conditions and the Life-Cycle of Societies (Kmtian Method and KA2 Law)**

| Core Concept | Core Principle | KA2 Function | Research Focus | Historical Insight |
|--------------|----------------|--------------|----------------|--------------------|
| Contradiction and unfolding (succession + concatenation) | Succession and concatenation of stages | Reveals the ordered unfolding of contradictions leading to transformation. | Trace how each social formation evolves from the contradictions of the previous one; study continuity and change. | Each mode of production (e.g., slavery → feudalism → capitalism) shows the maturation and decay of a social system. |
| Material specificity of each epoch | Epochal specificity | Emphasizes that every historical stage has unique laws determined by material conditions. | Identify the specific laws governing each epoch; reject universal or eternal laws of economy or politics. | Feudal land relations versus capitalist property relations—each corresponds to its period's needs. |
| Society as organism (the KA of living things) | Life cycle of social organisms | Demonstrates that social forms undergo birth, maturation, decline, REGRESSION before the leap, and death as natural historical processes. | Study the rise, peak, and decay of each system as an organism bound by its contradictions. | The Roman slave system declined; feudalism and global capitalism each decayed as new contradictions set in. |
| Transformation as leap (crisis → new mode rising) | Historical conditioning and transience | Marks the transformation point: the old becomes the foundation for the new. | Analyze how crises in one system lay the groundwork for a higher formation. | Capitalism’s technological base fuels both its contradictions and Maatic (balanced) potential for transformation. |

**Summary Process**
- Historical conditions determine the structure, character, and function of each social formation.
- Each stage unfolds from internal contradictions (seba), develops laws unique to its era (renpet), lives and dies like an organism (khet-ankh-mu), and becomes the foundation for the next order (benben).

**Stages of Social Development:**
- Birth → Growth → Contradiction → Crisis → Death → Rebirth (Negation of Negation).

The investigator must reveal the lawful succession of stages, identify the historical specificity of each, analyze its unique contradictions and cycle, and show how one formation becomes the basis for the next.

**Spiritual-Scientific Context**
- The process expresses *Maat* (order) and *Isfet* (negation) working together—truth emerging through transformation.
