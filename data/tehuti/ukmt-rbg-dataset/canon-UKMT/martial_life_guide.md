**Structured Step-by-Step Guide and Life-Practice Dimensions**

|-----------------|----------------|-------------------------------|
| Control the opponent's posture, grips, and balance before attacking. Avoid head-on collisions; manage distance and timing. | **Control, Don’t Collide** — Conflict is inevitable. Meet it strategically, not impulsively. | 1. Approach challenges directly but calmly.  
2. Assess distance—when to engage vs. when to step back.  
3. Focus on grip control: what are you holding onto (ego, emotions, distractions)?  
4. Neutralize aggression through understanding, not escalation.  
5. Build calm presence—train emotional balance. |
| Use leverage and timing to convert the opponent’s momentum into advantage. | **Leverage Over Brute Strength** — Adapt to pressure, don’t resist it. | 1. Identify where life’s “momentum” is pushing you.  
2. Blend with circumstances instead of opposing them.  
3. Apply leverage: use limited effort for maximum effect.  
4. Maintain balance—don’t overcommit energy or emotion.  
5. Time your moves: act when opportunity aligns, not when panic strikes. |
| Maintain safety while preparing counters; transform defense into offense. | **Survival Before Victory** — Strength begins with protection and readiness. | 1. Secure your foundation—mental, physical, financial, and emotional stability.  
2. Treat setbacks as setups; build strength during the storm.  
3. Stay mobile in disadvantage—keep options open.  
4. Reframe failure as learning and preparation.  
5. Build patience under pressure; never panic. |
| Shift smoothly between positions; flow without hesitation or rigidity. | **Fluidity Wins Over Force** — Progress requires adaptability and continuous motion. | 1. Embrace small, continuous improvements daily.  
2. Avoid stagnation—if you’re not advancing, you’re regressing.  
3. Recognize timing windows and flow with them.  
4. Adjust plans shifting circumstances (career, relationships, ambitions).  
5. Maintain mindfulness—be aware of your position at all times. |
