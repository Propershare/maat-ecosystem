## KMT-STYLE RESEARCH TEAM (SESHU N SIA)
### STRUCTURED COOPERATIVE FOR SCIENTIFIC-MORAL RECONSTRUCTION

| Transliteration       | Meaning / Symbolism                       | Step-by-Step: How to Build the Research Team (Seshu)                                                                                               |
|-----------------------|--------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **ḥtp**              | Peace, Order, Balance                     | Define the purpose: to restore scientific knowledge, moral order, and accurate historical reconstruction of African civilization. Clarify goals.    |
| **Mꜣꜥat**            | Truth, Justice, Right Order              | Declare the guiding principle of the team as **Mꜣꜥat**. Align all actions with moral clarity and harmony.                                           |
| **Mꜣꜥu (sš mdw)**     | Scribes of the Word                      | Select 3 core founders—one female, one male, one recorder. Must be morally clean, self-disciplined, and reconstruction-focused.                    |
| **ꜥedjat (pr)**        | House of the Scroll / Archive            | Write a **Seshu Handbook**—including mission, ethics, membership, and study goals. Each founder keeps a journal.                                     |
| **tꜢ ḥy**             | The People / Learners                    | Recruit within schools, communities, or families. Require a 3-page background statement. Interview for commitment.                                   |
| **mn**                | Pyramid / Ordered Structure              | Form a 3-member **Pyramid Committee (Per-Mer)**: Librarian, Recorder, and Co-Chairperson. Rotate quarterly.                                          |
| **pr ḥkꜢ (pr-ꜥnḫ)**   | House of Life / Temple of Learning       | Choose a clean, peaceful site in a home, school, or community. Treat it as a **Temple of Hand (Ḥepy)**.                                               |
| **sšw**               | Group of Scribes / Researchers           | Form teams of 3–5 members; when reaching 6, split into new teams. Require balance of genders. Each team focuses on a specific subject.              |
| **sia**               | Perception / Intelligence                | Meet **3 times per month**, 60–90 minutes each. Divide sessions: **Heart** (moral), **Hand** (practical), **Head** (intellectual).                  |
| **Seshu n Sia (sšw n sia)** | Researchers of Knowledge           | Begin with **historical-scientific reconstruction of Africa**. Assign mini projects—journals, debates, community interviews, oral histories.       |
| **Mꜣꜥat ꜥn mꜣꜥat**     | Knowledge of Truth                     | Develop a curriculum on science method, African history, moral philosophy, and health.                                                              |
| **ꜥib ꜥn mꜣꜥat**       | Heart in Truth                          | Attendance is mandatory. Harmony is sacred. Dismissal for arrogance, disrespect, or laziness.                                                       |
| **rekh sꜢ-ꜥnḫ**        | Enlightened Students                   | Share readings every quarter. Teach mutual support, dual-gender leadership, and service.                                                            |
| **rn**                | Name / Essence                          | Members declare their purpose. Names used for intent invocation.                                                                                    |
| **kheperu**           | Enduring Works / Stability              | Create long-term cooperative ventures, publications, and legacy tools.                                                                             |
| **pr ra (ḥwt ḥr)**    | House of the Day / Solar Temple         | Ritual: 1. Greet, 2. Circle of Principles, 3. Sign in, 4. Reading, 5. Agenda, 6. Truth-sharing, 7. Reflection, 8. Closing.                          |
| **sš mdw**            | Divine Words / Sacred Writing           | Record insights in journals. Publish findings. Submit reports monthly.                                                                             |
| **neter (mdw)**       | Shrines / Branch Temples                | Each new team is a **branch**. Coordinate monthly. Together, form a **Federation of Seshu n Sia**—a national network.                              |
