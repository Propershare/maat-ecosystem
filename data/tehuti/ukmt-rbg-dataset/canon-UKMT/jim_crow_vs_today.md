POST-RECONSTRUCTION WHITE JIM CROW VS. PRESENT-DAY 2025 WHITE NAZI REGRESSION

Category | Post-Reconstruction / Jim Crow Era (Late 1800s–1960s) | Present-Day (21st Century)
---|---|---
Voting Rights Suppression | Poll taxes, literacy tests, grandfather clauses, violence at the polls. | Voter ID laws, roll purges, gerrymandering, closing polling sites in Black neighborhoods, *Shelby v. Holder* (2013).
Terrorism Against Black People | Lynching, KKK violence, race massacres (e.g., Tulsa 1921). | Police brutality, hate crimes, white supremacist groups, racialized mass shootings, state surveillance of activists.
Economic Disenfranchisement | Sharecropping, denial of land ownership, exclusion from New Deal programs. | Persistent racial wage gap, higher Black unemployment, denial of loans, cuts to food stamps, WIC, housing subsidies.
Policing & Incarceration | Black Codes, convict leasing, chain gangs. | Mass incarceration, school-to-prison pipeline, qualified immunity, racial profiling, militarized policing.
Education Inequality | Segregated schools (*Plessy v. Ferguson*), underfunded Black schools. | De-facto school resegregation, attacks on public education, school choice policies harming Black students.
Health Inequities | Little to no access to hospitals or quality care in Black communities. | Cuts to Medicaid/Obamacare, maternal mortality crisis for Black women, medical racism.
Civil Rights Setbacks | Removal of federal protections for Black citizens post-1877. | Executive rollbacks of DEI initiatives, bans on affirmative action, weakened civil rights enforcement.
Censorship & Cultural Suppression | Prohibition of Black literacy and Black press in some states. | Book bans on race/gender topics, anti-CRT laws, attacks on “wokeness,” DEI programs dismantled.
Legalized Racism / Lack of Equal Protection | Supreme Court's *Plessy v. Ferguson* upheld segregation. | Weak enforcement of anti-discrimination laws, Supreme Court undermining protections (e.g., affirmative action, voting rights).
Public Narrative / Racial Justification | “Lost Cause” mythology, white supremacy normalized as social order. | “Colorblind”
