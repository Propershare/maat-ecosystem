# Scientific Journal Guidelines

## Content / Guideline

### Why Keep a KA2 Scientific Journal?
- Train scientific thinking
- Build fact-to-fact archiving
- Document contradictions & development
- Support personal growth

**Example/Notes:**
> "My notes track how ideas and facts evolve, not just isolated events."

### Process Orientation
- Everything is in motion
  - Move from simple → complex
  - From appearance → essence
  - From quantity → quality

**Example/Notes:**
> Record gradual shifts, note turning points, contrast earlier vs. later entries to identify emergent patterns.

### Historical Journaling
- Record exact facts & data
- Place in chronological order
- Compare fact-to-fact
- Draw insights after evidence accumulates

**Example/Notes:**
> TK copied statistics word-for-word, then compared across years to track economic shifts.

### Practical Rules
- Date entries
- Outline & numbering
- Summarize in own words
- Keep brief but structured

**Example/Notes:**
> Definitions and formulas written exactly

## Setting Up Journal
- Write name/date range
- Number pages
- Decide consistent abbreviations/symbols

**Example/Notes:**
> ∆ = change, → = leads to, ⊕ = contradiction, ↓ = decrease

## Standard Entry Structure
1. Date/Time/Context
2. Raw facts (brief)
3. Process/motion
4. Connections
5. Synthesis/reflection

**Example/Notes:**
> Lecture on African State Development: record contradictions, compare to earlier notes

## Practical Note Rules
- Briefness rule: word → phrase → sentence
- Exactness rule: copy formulas/facts exactly
- Outline rule: hierarchy with I, A, 1
- Catch-up rule: use keywords + blank space
- Margin rule: keywords/connections in margins
- Space rule: leave gaps for edits

**Example/Notes:**
> I. Surplus → stratification → irrigation coordination

## Post-entry Reflection
- Highlight key concepts
- Write summary (3–5 sentences)
- Cross-reference with earlier entries
- List new questions

**Example/Notes:**
> End of week: "This week shows environmental demand as driver of state centralization. Compare to 9/12 entry."

## Applied Sample
- **Facts:** Nile flood → resource → surplus, surplus → stratification, irrigation required coordination
- **Process:** Surplus accumulation vs. hierarchy contradiction
- **Contradiction:** Surplus due to (forces vs. relations), chronology
- **Synthesis:** Environment as driver of state formation
- **New Q:** How did market ideology stabilize contradictions?

## Daily/Weekly Routine
- Date & number notes
- Use standard structure
- Review last entry
- Link contradictions
